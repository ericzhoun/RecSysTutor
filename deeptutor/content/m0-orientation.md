# Module m0 — Orientation

## Orientation

Where the course is going, how to move through it, and the one mental model that makes every
      later model fall into place: the recommendation funnel.

### How to use this course

Recommender systems look intimidating because the literature is a pile of architectures. It is much easier once you
      sort every model by **which stage of the funnel it lives in** and **which problem it was invented to
      fix**. That is the organising principle here. Each lesson follows the same shape: the problem, the idea, the
      mechanics, and a practical takeaway.
- **Track A — Retrieval:** modules 2 and 6 (two-tower, FM, Twitter). Start here if your job is candidate generation.
- **Track B — Ranking:** module 3 (the CTR feature-interaction family). The densest, most practical module.
- **Track C — Sequence & generative:** module 4 (SASRec → BERT4Rec → HSTU). The frontier.
- **Track D — Multi-task:** module 5 (MMoE, ESMM, the optimiser zoo). The industry default.

> Prerequisites
      Comfort with feed-forward networks, embeddings, and one DL framework (the blog uses TensorFlow; PyTorch equivalents
        are noted). Softmax, cross-entropy, and basic probability are assumed. No prior recommender-systems knowledge required.
