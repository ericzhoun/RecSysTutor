Hey! I am Ludo.

I am a machine learning engineer at Google.

I have created "Machine learning at scale" to talk about ML systems in the real world. I find that the majority of online ML content is divided in these two groups:

  * Introduction to model X in Python.

  * An in-depth review of a new paper that improves metrics X by 0.001%.

However, what I am personally most interested in is how companies design and build ML systems that scale to billions.

I plan on deep diving in one major machine learning system every week.

Subscribe if you want to stay up to date with new ML system architectures.
