# **You read the title, so… What’s changing?**

I will cut right to the chase:

Lately, the act of writing posts felt more of a chore rather than a pleasure.

I believe that is also reflected in the quality of my posts. 

I have not been able to go as deep as I wanted to because of the idea in my mind that says: “Ah, I need to write something for this week post, I can’t spend multiple hours just reading this paper and then another hour to condense down my understanding, I need to be faster!”

But I started writing to go deeper into some topic that I care about and make that digestible for everyone!

So… I am going back to my roots! 

I will write something when I know the quality will satisfy my own standards and when I am sure I am adding value to the reader.

This means multiple things for you:

  * No more weekly newsletter. Emails will be random!

  * When a new article will be published, expect a longer article with more deep dives into what’s exactly going on.

  * Expect a wide array of topics that are not 100% focused on **Machine learning systems at scale**. If some day I want to learn all about some random math/ml/system topic that excites me, then that’s what you are going to get!   
An article full of excitement about something that I have learned and I want to share that with you. 

I hope that what I have wrote above resonates with what you want to get out of this newsletter.

And above all, thanks for being subscribed and reading my articles!

Ludo
