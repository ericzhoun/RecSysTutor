# Introduction

You might think: what a weird post to make in a time where getting offers is hard!

And to that I will say: “Yes and No!”

Plenty of candidates are getting top offers from different companies and are able to negotiate, while others are just getting no answers from applications.

Market is for sure weird. But top candidates are still able to pick and choose different offers and decide the comp. You should strive and be that top candidate.

And if you are, then this newsletter article is for you!

Back in late 2021, I was “that top candidate” (or/and the market was VERY good, i will let the reader decide which is which) and I was able to get a few offers at the same time. So I guess I want to share my thoughts!

This is my own personal opinion as always. I take no responsability if you negotiate too hard! ;)
